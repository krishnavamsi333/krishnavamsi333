## 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Power Electronics & Robotics Engineer** passionate about intelligent machines, clean energy systems, and real-time embedded control. My work bridges the physical and digital — from designing high-efficiency converters to building autonomous robots that navigate the real world.

- 🔋 Working on **BMS firmware**, **solar MPPT**, and **EV powertrain** systems
- 🤖 Building **ROS 2** robots with LiDAR-based autonomous navigation
- ⚡ Simulating and prototyping **DC-DC converters**, **inverters**, and **motor drives**
- 🏎️ **President** of the **Electric Vehicle Club** @ [VRSEC](https://siddhartha.edu.in/) — leading hands-on EV builds & workshops
- 📬 **kasimallakrishnavamsi333@gmail.com** | 🌐 **[Portfolio](https://krishnavamsi333.github.io/Portfolio/)**
